EasyEdu — IB / AP Tutoring

Instead of giving you the answer, EasyEdu asks you to explain it. That one shift — student explains, AI listens and questions — makes understanding stick.

How it works
Step 1

Pick a question

Browse questions organised by AP and IB course and chapter. Every question comes from the actual textbook.

Step 2

Explain your reasoning

Type your answer as if you're teaching a classmate. Don't just copy the formula — explain why it works.

Step 3

Get pushed deeper

If your answer is right but shallow, the system asks a follow-up. If it's wrong, it corrects the specific gap.
